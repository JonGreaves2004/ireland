IRELAND ROAD TRIP MAP PACK
20-27 September 2026

FILES
1. Ireland_Road_Trip_Interactive_Map.html
   Open in a web browser. It shows colour-coded daily layers and clickable markers.
   The connecting lines show the sequence of stops; they do not follow roads.

2. Daily_Google_Maps_Route_Links.html
   Open this on your phone or computer and choose the relevant day.
   Google Maps will calculate the live driving route.

3. Ireland_Road_Trip_Google_My_Maps_Import.csv
   Import into Google My Maps:
   - Open Google My Maps and create a new map.
   - Add a layer and choose Import.
   - Upload this CSV.
   - Select 'Address / Google Maps query' as the location column.
   - Select 'Name' as the marker title.
   - Style markers by the 'Day' column.
   You may create one layer per day by filtering/copying the rows.

4. Ireland_Road_Trip_Map.kml
   Import into Google My Maps, Google Earth or compatible navigation/mapping apps.
   Coordinates are approximate planning coordinates; always use live navigation for driving.
